# Naval academy course tracker

Owner direction: restrained navy, warm paper, clear course hierarchy.
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Navy anchors headings and actions to give the catalogue a formal academy identity.
- Warm paper supports sustained reading and course comparison as the fixed theme.
- Existing serif headings retain naval character; sans serif body text supports scanning.
- Flat course records let titles and prerequisites carry the hierarchy.
- Status colors communicate actual completion, waiting, progress, and errors only.
- Spacing separates catalogue groups while controls remain compact and predictable.
- Motion provides interaction feedback without animated decoration.
- Existing insignia is retained without glow; no new assets are introduced.

## TRMN brand nudge

Reference: [TRMN Organizational Style Guide, 2020](https://trmn.org/user/pages/07.forms-and-manuals/06.manuals/03.misc-manuals/_organizational-style-guide/2020%20Revised%20Organizational%20Style%20Guide%204-1.pdf).

- A restrained TRMN red masthead rule identifies the organization while navy remains the interface color.
- The existing seal sits flat to keep its artwork crisp on paper.
- The subtitle is upright and the masthead tracking is normal for a more formal reading hierarchy.
- Gold stays in the existing seal rather than becoming low-contrast text on paper.
- The guide names Decalotype, Akrobat, and Droid Serif in its presentation guidance. Existing Cinzel and Roboto remain a deliberate app interpretation, not a claim of official typography compliance.
- The owner's navy and paper direction remains primary; this is a modest brand adaptation rather than a reproduction of TRMN's dark presentation palette.
